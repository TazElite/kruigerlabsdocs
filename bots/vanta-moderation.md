# Vanta Moderation & AutoMod
Vanta includes moderation and AutoMod functionality as part of its all-in-one feature set.

## Before enabling enforcement
Place Vanta's role correctly, give only required Discord permissions, configure staff roles, and test actions on a non-staff test account.

## Dashboard controls
A guild can disable modules or individual commands. If moderation appears unavailable, verify the Moderation/AutoMod module and command switches before changing Discord permissions.

## Safety
Do not give ordinary staff permissions beyond their job. Test kick/ban/timeout or destructive actions in a controlled environment before relying on them operationally.
