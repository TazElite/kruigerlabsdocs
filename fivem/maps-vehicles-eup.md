# Maps, Vehicles & EUP Organization
Streamed assets deserve their own organization because they can become a large part of a server.

## Suggested categories

```text
resources/
├── [maps]/
├── [vehicles]/
└── [eup]/
```

## Add in batches
Do not upload dozens of unknown packs and troubleshoot them all at once. Add a small batch, boot, check console/client behavior, and test.

## Conflicts
Two resources can replace the same game asset or define conflicting metadata. If a problem begins after a new pack, disable the newest batch and narrow it down.

## Success check
Every asset pack has a known source/version and can be disabled independently during troubleshooting.
