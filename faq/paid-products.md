# Kruiger Paid Products FAQ
<details><summary><strong>Can I install the Ultimate Suite over similar resources?</strong></summary><p>Plan the replacement first. Back up the server and disable conflicting HUD/chat/priority/headtag systems only when the Kruiger replacements are configured and ready.</p></details>
<details><summary><strong>Why should department IDs match?</strong></summary><p>Duty, HUD, Headtags and Chat integrations can refer to the same department. Stable internal identifiers prevent one component from referring to a department differently from another.</p></details>
<details><summary><strong>Can I share my paid resource files?</strong></summary><p>Follow the product license and delivery terms. Do not upload paid files to a public repository merely to deploy them.</p></details>
<details><summary><strong>Should I overwrite my old config during an update?</strong></summary><p>No. Back it up and compare the new default/config schema with your existing settings.</p></details>
<details><summary><strong>Why do the docs say to use my installed version's exact ACE?</strong></summary><p>Granular permission names can change between releases. The documentation avoids inventing a suffix that may not exist in your version.</p></details>
