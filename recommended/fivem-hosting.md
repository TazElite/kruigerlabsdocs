## Before buying any host
Check:
1. Location close to the majority of your players.
2. Strong CPU performance for your workload.
3. Enough RAM for framework/assets/player count.
4. Database availability.
5. SFTP/file access.
6. Backups and restore process.
7. DDoS protection.
8. Support availability.
9. Upgrade path.
10. Refund/terms and acceptable-use policies.

## Sizing
Do not buy solely from a generic “players = RAM” chart. Framework, streamed assets, scripts, database workload, and badly optimized resources can dramatically change requirements. Start from the provider's guidance, measure the real server, and scale when needed.
