# Troubleshooting Technician Path

**Level:** Intermediate  
**Estimated hands-on time:** 2–4 hours

## Prerequisites
Use a test environment whenever a lesson changes server, bot, database or permissions. Keep backups and never paste production secrets into tutorials or support messages.

## Capstone
Take a deliberately broken test resource, isolate the fault without random changes, fix it, prove the fix after a cold boot, and produce a complete support report.

## Completion standard
You are finished when the capstone works after a full restart/reconnect and you can explain how to roll it back.
