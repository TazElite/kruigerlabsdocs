## Capstone
Configure a test server from scratch, verify a normal member, open/close a ticket, generate expected logs, test disabled commands/modules, and confirm role hierarchy.

## Completion standard
You are finished when the capstone works after a full restart/reconnect and you can explain how to roll it back.
