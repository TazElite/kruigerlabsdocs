**Level:** Beginner  
**Estimated hands-on time:** 2–4 hours

## Prerequisites

## Capstone
Build a small standalone resource, install it on your own test server, protect an administrative action server-side, cold-boot the server, and document how to install/rollback it.

## Completion standard
You are finished when the capstone works after a full restart/reconnect and you can explain how to roll it back.
